ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

Welcome to Along Digital

Your Digital File Space — a simple and centralized platform to host, manage and share your images and documents.

Who We Are

Along Digital is a digital platform designed to make file hosting and file management easier. Our goal is to provide a convenient space where users can upload images, store documents and generate shareable URLs for their websites and digital projects.

What We Provide

▧

Image Hosting

Upload images and generate public URLs for use in websites and applications.

▤

Document Hosting

Store documents and create shareable download links.

▣

File Management

Manage hosted files from one centralized dashboard.

Our Technology

The platform is designed around modern web technologies. Supabase will provide storage and database services, while Google Sheets will maintain an additional record of uploaded files and their URLs.
